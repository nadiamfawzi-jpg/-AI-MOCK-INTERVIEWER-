AI INTERVIEW COACH — SHORT DEMO GUIDE

1. PROBLEM AND PURPOSE
"Many candidates know their subject but do not have an accessible way to practise both answer content and visible delivery before a real interview. AI Interview Coach provides guided questions, instant answer feedback, and video-based practice cues."

2. START THE DEMO
- Point to the four colourful steps.
- Choose a field, question, and confidence level from the sidebar.

3. MOCK INTERVIEW
- Enter an answer and click Check My Answer.
- Explain the relevance score, key ideas, strengths, missing ideas, and sample answer.

4. VIDEO COACH
- Both live webcam and uploaded video are supported.
- YOLO detects the person; MediaPipe supplies face and hand landmarks.
- The results are visible cues only, not a person's true emotion.

5. SUMMARY
- Show the overall practice score and next goal.
- This is a coaching score, not a recruitment decision.

6. TERMS
- NLP: Natural Language Processing — analyses human language.
- TF-IDF: Term Frequency–Inverse Document Frequency — gives words numerical importance.
- Cosine Similarity: measures how similar two text vectors are.
- YOLO: You Only Look Once — detects the person in video frames.
- OpenCV: Open Source Computer Vision Library — processes video frames.
- MediaPipe: supplies face and hand landmarks.

7. IF ASKED ABOUT THE TUTOR'S CODE
"I followed the Ultralytics YOLO and OpenCV pattern from the supplied notebooks: load YOLO, pass each frame to the model, and use results[0].plot(). The tutor did not provide Streamlit, emotion, gesture, or answer-scoring exercises, so I added those parts to complete the project."

8. CLOSING
"The project combines answer quality and delivery practice in one supportive tool. Future improvements could include speech-to-text and models trained on suitable emotion or gesture datasets."
